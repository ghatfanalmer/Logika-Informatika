Sangkuriang & Tangkuban Perahu

Sangkuriang & Mount Tangkuban Perahu

Sangkuriang, seorang pemuda sakti, tanpa sadar jatuh cinta pada ibu kandungnya sendiri, Dayang Sumbi. Untuk menggagalkan niat pernikahan tersebut, Dayang Sumbi memberinya syarat mustahil: membendung sungai dan membuat perahu besar hanya dalam satu malam.

Sangkuriang, a young man with magical powers, unknowingly fell in love with his own biological mother, Dayang Sumbi. To thwart the marriage, Dayang Sumbi gave him an impossible condition: dam a river and build a massive boat in just one night.

Saat Sangkuriang hampir berhasil, Dayang Sumbi memalsukan datangnya fajar. Karena marah merasa gagal, Sangkuriang menendang perahu buatannya hingga terbalik, yang kini dikenal sebagai Gunung Tangkuban Perahu.

When Sangkuriang almost succeeded, Dayang Sumbi faked the break of dawn. Enraged by his perceived failure, Sangkuriang kicked his boat upside down, which is now known as Mount Tangkuban Perahu.

Malin Kundang & Kutukan Batu

Malin Kundang & The Stone Curse

Kisah seorang anak miskin dari Minangkabau yang merantau dan sukses menjadi saudagar kaya raya. Namun, saat kapalnya bersandar kembali ke kampung halamannya, ia malu dan enggan mengakui ibu kandungnya yang renta dan miskin.

The story of a poor boy from Minangkabau who migrated and succeeded in becoming a wealthy merchant. However, when his ship docked back in his hometown, he was ashamed and refused to acknowledge his elderly, poor mother.

Sang ibu yang sakit hati akhirnya memanjatkan doa. Seketika badai datang, kapal hancur disambar petir, dan Malin Kundang dikutuk berubah menjadi batu karang yang bersujud, memohon ampunan yang tak pernah ia dapatkan.

The heartbroken mother finally prayed to God. Instantly, a storm arrived, the ship was destroyed by lightning, and Malin Kundang was cursed and turned into a prostrating rock, begging for forgiveness he would never receive.
